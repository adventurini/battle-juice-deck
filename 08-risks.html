The honest
risk picture

A direct read on what could go wrong, scaled by likelihood and impact. The biggest risk is commercial — generating broad, repeatable velocity — not operational.

Velocity / sell-through at scale

The central risk. Early cashier-placement sell-through at Horrocks and Vince & Joe's is encouraging, but a national retail partner requires broad, repeatable scan velocity across many doors. Failing to scale velocity stalls the entire retail thesis.

High

Distribution / retail partnership

Securing an exclusive national retail partner (DSG) within 12 months is the key commercial dependency, and is downstream of proving velocity.

Med-High

Production / manufacturing run

There is always a minor risk a drink batch or run fails. Drinks are produced by Yaya, a $10B+ facility, making realized risk exceptionally low. Runs are also unpaid, so timing/scale stay flexible.

Low

Founder compensation funding

The salary-to-equity swap is contingent on founders securing personal financial means. Until then, founder cash comp remains a fixed monthly cost.

Low-Med

Net read: Operational and product risk is low, and unpaid manufacturing keeps cash flexible. The decisive variables are commercial — scaling velocity beyond the first accounts, and converting that data into a national retail launch.
